# Audit Single-Purpose Principle

*Established: 2026-07-30. Refined by the principal security and privacy architect.*
*Authority: This principle supersedes any conflicting configuration, feature, or implementation decision.*

---

## Ground principle

Every audit record generated by Verbatim — including transcript derivation trees, audit event
logs, job metadata, export records, and deletion records — exists for **one purpose only**:

> **Product defensibility**: proving that Verbatim processed authorized audio within its stated
> boundaries, using the qualified model and pipeline, with correct deletion and export behavior,
> so that the organization can defend its records-handling practices to authorized auditors,
> regulators, or legal reviewers.

No other use is permitted. This is not a default — it is a covenant.

---

## What this prohibits, without exception

The following uses are prohibited regardless of who requests them, at any level of the
organization, without a separate legal authorization reviewed by the principal security and
privacy architect:

| Prohibited use | Rationale |
|----------------|-----------|
| Product analytics or usage telemetry | Audit records are not product data |
| Accuracy benchmarking or model evaluation | Audit records are not evaluation corpora |
| AI model training, fine-tuning, or distillation | Audit records are not training data |
| Transfer to any AI provider, including Anthropic | Audit records are proprietary to the organization |
| Commercial use or licensing of audit content | Audit records are internal compliance records |
| Cross-job aggregation for business intelligence | Audit records are per-job, not a dataset |
| Sharing with third parties not authorized to receive records | Audit records follow records-access policy, not app-access policy |
| Use by the application for normal operation | The transcript API must never read the audit store |

---

## What this permits

The following uses are the **complete list** of authorized purposes:

1. Responding to an authorized records audit or legal hold for a specific job.
2. Verifying chain of custody during an incident investigation for the organization.
3. Satisfying a regulatory inspection that requires evidence of processing integrity.
4. Supporting a security review by the organization's authorized security function.

All permitted uses require explicit authorization per query. The audit query endpoint
(`STS_AUDIT_QUERY_ENABLED`) is off by default and requires a named authorization record.

---

## Enforcement

This principle is enforced at four layers:

| Layer | Mechanism |
|-------|-----------|
| Architecture | ADR-007: separate encrypted store; all application paths categorically excluded from read access |
| Code | Audit subsystem is the only writer; export handlers assert zero audit bytes in response (negative-control test) |
| QA | PQAPS validates no-export guard, purpose-limitation marker, and HMAC integrity before QG-03/QG-05 close |
| Governance | Records/privacy owner and principal security and privacy architect must sign the retention scope and purpose statement before the audit store is enabled |

---

## Non-waiver clause

No role below the principal security and privacy architect may grant an exception to this
principle. No product requirement, business case, customer request, or performance target
constitutes an exception. If a use case appears to require access to audit records for a
purpose not listed above, the request must be reviewed as a separate legal and governance
matter — it does not modify this principle.

---

## Scope of application

This principle applies to:
- Transcript derivation tree records (STS-123)
- Audit event log entries (existing content-free audit events)
- Job lifecycle metadata (creation, status, deletion timestamps)
- Export destination records
- Any future structured record generated by Verbatim about how audio was processed

It does not apply to the transcript content itself (governed by the organization's records
and DLP policy) or to export artifacts delivered to the operator (governed by ADR-003).
